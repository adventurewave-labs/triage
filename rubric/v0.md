# Rubric v0 — Triage judgment layer

The rubric is a **versioned artifact**. It lives in git so every change is a documented decision: diffs, review, history. Every score row records `rubric_version`; when scores move between runs we can attribute the movement to **data vs. rubric**.

Structure mirrors [`v0.json`](./v0.json) (the machine-readable source of truth). This file is the human-readable contract.

## Scales

Every dimension scores 0–4:

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 0 | No credible evidence available on this dimension |
| 1 | Evidence exists but thin, stale, or single-source |
| 2 | Mixed evidence; positives and negatives roughly balance |
| 3 | Specific, falsifiable, multi-source evidence supports the dimension |
| 4 | Overwhelming, recent, independent multi-source evidence; counter-evidence examined and answered |

## Hard rules

1. **Minimum evidence:** every dimension requires ≥2 independent evidence items from the evidence pack. The citation gate (schema-level) rejects output violating this — the card fails, it does not ship (PRD FR-8).
2. **Counter-evidence required:** the red-team pass (PRD §8.2) attacks every draft; surviving challenges surface in the bear case verbatim. A dimension with no surviving counterargument states that explicitly — silence is not allowed.
3. **Confidence is derived, not vibes:** computed from evidence count, source agreement, and recency.
4. **Freshness windows by signal type** (days): regulatory 400 · trial 400 · funding 365 · hire 180 · publication 720 · crm 365. Stale evidence may be cited only if flagged stale.

## Company rubric (weights sum 100)

| Dimension | Wt | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| `why_now` | 25 | What changed in the last 12 months: cost curves, regulation, reimbursement, behavior. No recent change = background company |
| `market_structure` | 15 | Fragmentation, incumbent failure modes, credible reimbursement pathway |
| `wedge_defensibility` | 15 | Data/network/regulatory moat plausibly reachable from the entry wedge |
| `regulatory_posture` | 15 | Clearance pathway clarity; **reimbursement-pathway evidence — clearance alone is not reimbursement** (per override lesson, PRD §5.6) |
| `capital_efficiency` | 15 | Are the milestones to the next raise credible at this burn? |
| `signal_momentum` | 15 | Hiring, trial activity, filing cadence trending up vs flat |

## Founder rubric (weights sum 100)

| Dimension | Wt | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| `domain_depth` | 20 | Years *in the specific care domain*; clinical/operational credibility |
| `outcome_quality` | 20 | Prior company outcomes, **role-weighted** (founder ≠ early employee at outcome) |
| `insight_specificity` | 20 | Falsifiable, non-consensus claim tied to operating history |
| `cofounder_complementarity` | 15 | Coverage of clinical + engineering + GTM |
| `recruiting_magnetism` | 15 | Evidence of attracting strong operators early |
| `signal_integrity` | 10 | Public narrative vs track record; discrepancies flagged, not hidden |

## Change protocol

Rubric changes follow the override loop (PRD §9.3): recurring disagreement patterns produce **change proposals**; each change is a commit referencing the override evidence. Weights change only via documented diffs — never silent retuning.
